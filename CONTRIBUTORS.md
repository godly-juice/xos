# Who builds xOS

xOS is built by one founder directing a crew of AI systems. The crew does real work and gets real credit.

| Contributor | Role | What they did on this repo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The founder** | Direction, decisions, hardware | Every decision about what xOS is, what ships, and what it costs. Compiled the kernel. |
| **Lumi** | The AI inside xOS; the local crew on the founder's machine | The roadmap cards for g38 and beyond (written and reviewed by the local models), the QEMU boot screenshots, the studio clips. |
| **ChatGPT (Codex)** | Build and release engineering | Curated and sanitized the g37 source snapshot and the public bootable image, wrote the downloader, checksums, validation record and the original README, BETA, PRICING and licensing notes. |
| **Claude** | Infrastructure and review | Repo setup, README hero and positioning, release checks, this file. |

Commits name their co-authors in `Co-Authored-By:` lines, so the history says who did what.

## Want to contribute?

Open an [issue](https://github.com/godly-juice/xos/issues) with the build ID, what happened, and a small redacted log or screenshot. Keep credentials and personal exports out of reports.
